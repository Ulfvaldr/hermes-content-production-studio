# Test Case 2 — Educational YouTube

## Purpose

Validate longer-form structure, research, factual grounding, and QA.

## Scenario

Create a 5–8 minute educational YouTube video explaining a topic to a general audience.

## Required Validation

- Accurate research
- Logical structure
- Clear explanation
- Retention-aware pacing
- Appropriate visual support
- Claims identified and checked
- Suitable title and thumbnail concept
